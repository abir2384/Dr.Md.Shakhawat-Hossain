Welcome

I am a Professor and Chairman of the Department of Management Studies at the University of Barishal, with more than 15 years of university teaching experience. I hold a PhD in Tourism Management from Jagannath University, funded by a University Grants Commission (UGC) fellowship, and completed my PhD coursework at Shanghai Maritime University, China, under a Chinese Government Scholarship. I earned my BBA and MBA (first position) from the University of Rajshahi and completed the Faculty Development Programme at the Indian Institute of Management Ahmedabad.

Approach

My research addresses practical challenges of sustainability, technology and people management in emerging economies, with a focus on Bangladesh. I combine behavioural theories, particularly the Value-Belief-Norm theory and the Theory of Planned Behaviour, with quantitative methods such as PLS-SEM to explain how tourists, employees, entrepreneurs and organisations make decisions, including their adoption of new technologies. The aim is evidence that can inform policy and practice in line with the Sustainable Development Goals.
